# FlyCut identity and brand policy / 名称与品牌政策

The product-name and branding conditions in [LICENSE](LICENSE), section 3, are binding conditions of the custom community permission. This explanation does not grant standard AGPL rights or permission to remove those conditions.

1. **Do not rename FlyCut Caption.** Community forks and private modifications must retain the product name and existing visible FlyCut Caption logos, product-name notices and attribution. Do not substitute another product identity or conceal it in interfaces or distributed documentation.
2. **Distinguish third-party versions.** Keep the name and add a qualifier such as “FlyCut Caption — Unofficial modified version by [operator]”. Identify changes or the actual operator; do not imply official endorsement, support or partnership without permission.
3. **Technical identifiers may change.** A fork's repository URL, package namespace or build ID may change to avoid collisions, but this does not authorize changing the displayed product identity. Independent host applications may keep their own name while visibly preserving the incorporated FlyCut Caption identity.
4. **Preserve legal notices.** Retain applicable copyright, license and third-party notices. Do not misrepresent authorship.
5. **Payment does not authorize rebranding.** A commercial integration license does not automatically waive name retention. Any exception requires explicit written permission from the relevant rights holder.
6. **Media and references.** No watermark is required on users' videos or subtitles. Truthful descriptions of origin or compatibility are permitted; the limited branding display permission does not authorize unrelated products to adopt FlyCut branding.

These rules concern rights controlled by FlyCut Team, do not assert registration of any mark and do not eliminate mandatory statutory rights. They explain a custom software-license condition, not a claim that trademark law alone prohibits all renaming.

# Commercial licensing / 商业授权

This document describes the commercial licensing process. **It is not a commercial grant or signed customer agreement.** Without a separate agreement, the custom community terms in [LICENSE](LICENSE) govern covered project code.

## When commercial authorization is required

**Closed-source integration requires a prior written commercial license.** This includes proprietary applications embedding, linking or bundling the React library or other covered code while withholding required Corresponding Source or imposing incompatible proprietary terms. It includes private/internal closed-source integrations as defined in LICENSE section 1.

A written commercial license is also required to operate a modified covered service without providing the source required by the community license, or for other requested exceptions to its conditions. Contact **x007xyzabc@gmail.com** with the relevant code/version, integration architecture, distribution/deployment scope and requested exceptions.

Ordinary business use of the standalone tool, monetized video production and charging for compliant services do not by themselves require a paid software license. Independent programs only communicating through API, CLI or MCP do not automatically become integrations of the code because their own source is private.

## Name retention is the default

**Commercial integration permission does not automatically permit renaming FlyCut Caption or removing branding.** Name retention is the default for community and commercial offerings. Any exception requires explicit written permission from the relevant rights holder; do not infer it from payment or proprietary integration permission.

## Written agreement and rights

An agreement must identify the actual contracting parties, covered code and versions, permitted products and deployments, exact exceptions, fees, term, support, warranties and termination provisions. No fixed price is published here; downloading the repository does not grant commercial exceptions.

FlyCut Team can license only rights it owns or is authorized to offer commercially. Third-party software and assets keep their own licenses. Contributions lacking sufficient alternative-licensing rights must be excluded, replaced or separately authorized before inclusion in a commercial grant. Review rights and contractual terms before signing.

## Hosted services are separate

Official ASR, translation, rendering and storage payments purchase those services. They do not purchase a closed-source integration license, a name/brand waiver or a change to downloaded software terms. A software commercial license does not include unlimited hosted usage unless expressly agreed.

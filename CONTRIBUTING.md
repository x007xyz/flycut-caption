# Contributing and licensing

Thank you for contributing to FlyCut Caption. The current community license is the **FlyCut Caption Community Source License 1.0**, a custom source-available license with name-retention and source-sharing conditions. Read [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) before submitting changes. It is not standard AGPL or an OSI-approved open-source license.

When submitting a contribution, identify its authors and any incorporated third-party material and license notices. Confirm that you have the rights or your employer's permission needed to submit it under the community license. Preserve third-party notices. Do not include secrets or material you cannot license.

Submitting a contribution does **not** transfer copyright to FlyCut Team and does **not**, by itself, grant permission to offer that contribution under a proprietary commercial license. The project has not adopted an automatic copyright assignment or contributor license agreement through this document.

Because the project offers separate commercial licensing for rights it controls, maintainers must record explicit alternative-licensing permission from the relevant rights holders before including an external contribution in that offering. If permission is unavailable, track the contribution as community-only and exclude or replace it in the commercial offering. A community-only contribution may still be considered for the community edition. Do not incorporate third-party material whose license is incompatible with this custom license's additional restrictions.

Maintainers should record the contribution commit, rights holder, applicable license, permission evidence, and commercial scope in the release rights review. A bot identity, pull-request approval, or Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off alone is not proof of proprietary relicensing rights.
